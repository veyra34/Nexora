# Problem Statement

## Problem

Competitive programmers participate in contests hosted across many online platforms. Each platform uses its own schedule, announcement channels, and account handles, so finding and tracking relevant contests and personal participation becomes fragmented and time-consuming. Programmers often lose track of upcoming contests, miss registration or start times, and struggle to keep a consolidated record of past performance and problem references. Managing multiple platform handles and remembering which problems, editorials, or resources relate to which contest adds cognitive overhead and reduces time available for practice and preparation.

## Target Users

This system is intended for competitive programmers and students who regularly take part in online programming contests. It is aimed at individuals who need a single, user-focused view of their contest activities and coding-platform profiles, including those preparing for competitive programming courses, campus contests, or online ladders.

## Proposed Solution

Contestify provides a single, user-centered place where competitive programmers can manage contest-related information and their coding-platform profiles. From the user perspective, Contestify lets you gather contest listings and the contest-related details that matter to you, and keeps a personal record of the handles and profiles you use across different coding sites. Instead of switching between platforms to look up schedules, results, or profile information, you use Contestify to view and organize the contest entries and profile identifiers that are important to your practice and competition calendar. The system focuses on helping users maintain a consistent, accessible summary of their contest engagements and public handles so that planning, review, and profile verification are straightforward and reliable.

## Expected Outcome

Using Contestify reduces the fragmentation that competitive programmers face when managing contests and platform identities. Users will spend less time searching multiple websites for schedules or account details and more time on meaningful preparation. Contestify's consolidated view makes it easier to find upcoming contests, recall which handles were used for past events, and gather the contest-specific materials that support study and post-contest review. Overall, users gain a clearer, more organized workflow for discovering contests, tracking participation, and managing their competitive-programming identities in one place.
